A Letter From Your Children

To the two people who gave us our world,

Before we understood what love meant, we were already surrounded by yours.

Before we knew what sacrifice looked like, you were already making sacrifices for us. Before we understood how difficult life could be, you were doing your best to make ours a little easier.

We may never fully understand every silent sacrifice, every worry you kept to yourselves, or every dream you set aside so that we could have a better future. But as we grow older, we understand more and more how precious your love truly is.

Baba Maa, thank you for giving us life, for giving us a home, for teaching us through your own journey that love is not simply a feeling. It is patience, forgiveness, commitment, kindness and choosing one another even when life is difficult.

Your twenty-four years together are so much more than a number. They are the foundation of our family, the warmth of our home and one of the greatest blessings of our lives.

On this special day, we wish we could give you something as beautiful as everything you have given us. Perhaps these little moments cannot repay your love, but we hope they remind you of how deeply you are loved and appreciated.

May the years ahead bring you more reasons to smile together, more memories to treasure, more strength through every challenge and more peaceful mornings beside one another.

Thank you for bringing us into this world. Thank you for being our parents. Thank you for showing us that a beautiful life becomes even more beautiful when it is shared with someone you love.

Happy 24th Anniversary, Baba Maa.

With all the love in our hearts, Bivor & Bornil ♡
